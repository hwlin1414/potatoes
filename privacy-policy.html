Privacy Policy

Potatoes VA ("Potatoes VA", "we", "us", or "our") is a virtual airline and online aviation community created for flight simulation purposes.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Potatoes VA, we may collect:

  • Name or display name
  • IVAO VID
  • Email address
  • Discord username or user ID
  • Membership and flight activity information
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser information and access logs
  • Other information voluntarily provided by you

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for operating the VA and providing our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your Potatoes VA membership
  • Verify your IVAO account
  • Maintain pilot and operational records
  • Process flight reports or other VA activities
  • Communicate with members
  • Maintain website security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with applicable laws and network policies

3. IVAO Information

Potatoes VA may process information relating to your IVAO account in order to provide virtual airline services.

IVAO are separate organizations and maintain their own privacy policies. Members should review the applicable privacy policies of those networks.

4. Third-Party Services

Potatoes VA may use third-party services such as:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Discord
  • Flight tracking or VA management platforms
  • Email providers
  • Analytics services

Such services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Public Information

Some information may be displayed publicly as part of normal VA operations, such as:

  • Pilot name or display name
  • IVAO VID where applicable
  • Awards or achievements

We will avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law or necessary to resolve disputes or enforce our rules.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information about how your data is processed

Requests may be submitted using our official contact details.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Our website should use HTTPS for all connections involving personal information.

9. Cookies

Our website may use necessary cookies to provide website functionality.

If we use non-essential analytics or tracking cookies, we will provide appropriate information and consent mechanisms where required.

10. Children's Privacy

Potatoes VA is a flight simulation community. Membership requirements may be subject to applicable age restrictions imposed by the services or networks used by our members.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy-related requests, please contact:

Potatoes VA Website: https://www.potatoes-va.com/

This Privacy Policy applies to Potatoes VA services only and does not replace or override the privacy policies of IVAO, Discord, or other third-party services.
