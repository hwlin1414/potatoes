About Us

Welcome to Potatoes VA

Potatoes VA is a virtual airline created by flight simulation enthusiasts who simply enjoy flying.

Our goal is simple: to provide a friendly, relaxed and enjoyable environment where pilots can fly together, discover new places, and experience online aviation on IVAO.

Whether you are an experienced online pilot or just getting started, you are welcome at Potatoes VA.

Rules & Regulations

Please read and follow these rules as a member of Potatoes VA.

1. General

Potatoes VA is a virtual aviation community created for flight simulation purposes only. Membership is free and does not represent employment, commercial aviation or any real-world airline operation.

2. Network Rules

All members must comply with the applicable rules, regulations and policies of IVAO while connected to their networks. Where a network rule conflicts with a Potatoes VA rule, the applicable network rule takes priority.

3. Conduct

Members are expected to behave respectfully and professionally. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disruptive behavior, or intentionally interfering with network operations is not permitted.

4. Flight Operations

Members should operate flights in a realistic and responsible manner. Pilots must follow applicable air traffic control instructions and network procedures while connected.

5. Callsigns

Members should use the callsign(s) registered for Potatoes VA when operating VA flights on the applicable network.

6. Copyright & Software

Pirated software, unauthorized copyrighted material, charts, documents, aircraft files or other protected material must not be distributed through Potatoes VA resources. Members are responsible for complying with the licenses of the software and content they use.

7. Membership

Potatoes VA reserves the right to suspend or terminate membership where a member seriously or repeatedly violates these rules or applicable network policies.

8. Changes

Potatoes VA may update these rules when necessary. Members are responsible for reviewing the current version of the rules.
